Old Gold Bike Store Management System

Project Description

Developed a Python-based Old Gold Bike Store Management System that allows customers to register, log in, browse vintage and classic Indian motorcycles, view bike specifications, purchase bikes, generate bills, make payments, and receive purchase invoices through WhatsApp.

The system simulates a real-world old bike showroom by providing bike catalogs, customer management, OTP verification, QR code payment, invoice generation, PDF bill creation, and multimedia-based bike presentation.
