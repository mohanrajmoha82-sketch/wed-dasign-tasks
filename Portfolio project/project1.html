RiskAssure - Digital Insurance Suite

Project Description

Developed a Python-based Medical Insurance Management System that allows users to register, log in, compare insurance policies, apply for government and private insurance schemes, generate insurance IDs, process payments, and receive insurance details through WhatsApp.

The system simulates a real-world health insurance application process by providing policy selection, OTP verification, premium calculation, QR-based UPI payment, digital insurance cards, and customer profile management.
